How Anti-Cheating Works

Lab layout & neighbor restriction rules

🛡️ 8-Neighbor Workstation Exclusion

When assigning a project to Seat #X, the algorithm queries all 8 adjacent workstations (Front, Back, Left, Right, and 4 Diagonals) within that 5x5 wing. The chosen student will NEVER receive the same project as their neighboring classmates!
